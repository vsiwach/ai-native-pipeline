02 · WHAT MODULAR DOES

Three mechanisms, one machine

THE RIGHT CHIP FOR EACH JOB

Split serving into its two phases — disaggregation — and run each on the silicon that does it cheapest. Output generation runs on memory-rich chips: up to 40% lower cost per token, independently measured.

ONE SOFTWARE LAYER, EVERY CHIP

Compiler technology from the team behind Swift and LLVM runs the same model on NVIDIA, AMD, or Qualcomm — no rewrite, no lock-in. First to the economics of every chip.

THEIR DATA STAYS HOME

High-volume tasks run on open-weight models the customer controls, inside their own cloud. Frontier models only through a controlled gate. Frontier quality where needed; 10× cheaper everywhere else.

IN ONE SENTENCE

Modular runs a company’s AI tasks at the lowest proven cost per task, on whatever silicon is cheapest that month, with the company’s data and know-how staying inside its own walls.
